# Feature Tour

## First launch

A four-page **welcome tour** plays over the animated weather background: clear skies, then rain, then a night thunderstorm. The last page asks for location and notification permission in one tap. You can replay it from Settings.

## ☀️ Now

- **Place header.** Shows "My Location · Near Macon", or a chosen city. Tap it to pick from 22 Georgia places grouped by region (Metro Atlanta, North Georgia Mountains, Coastal Georgia, Golden Isles…), with search.
- **Living background.** The sky animates to match current conditions: rain streaks, drifting clouds, snow, twinkling stars at night, a pulsing sun, and lightning bolts during thunderstorms.
- **Alert banner.** Shows the most dangerous active alert in its severity color. Tap it to jump to Alerts.
- **Hero.** Big temperature, condition, high/low and feels-like.
- **Quick actions.** Live Radar, Report Outage, Tropics, Lightning Timer, Be Ready.
- **Storm callout.** Shown when a tropical system is active. It has a spinning hurricane icon, category, winds and distance from you.
- **Next 24 hours**, and a **7-day forecast** with Apple-Weather-style range bars.
- **Detail tiles:**
  - **Muggy Meter:** dew-point comfort from "Crisp" to "Swamp Mode"
  - Wind, with a direction arrow
  - UV index
  - Air quality (US AQI)
  - **Pollen estimate** (Georgia's spring pine pollen, summer grass, fall ragweed)
  - Sunrise/sunset, humidity, pressure

## 🗺️ Radar

- A full-screen map starts framed on the whole state.
- **Animated radar loop:** about 2 hours of past frames, plus nowcast frames in peach. Play/pause, scrub with the slider, time label, intensity legend.
- **Controls:** zoom in, zoom out, **zoom to my location**, show all of Georgia, and report an outage.
- **Layers sheet:** radar on/off and opacity, warning polygons, hurricanes and cones, power outages. Map styles are Standard (muted, so radar colors pop), Hybrid and Satellite.
- **Tap a warning polygon** to open that alert. **Tap a storm** to open storm detail.

## ⚠️ Alerts

- Live NWS alerts for Georgia, sorted: Extreme → Severe → …, then warnings → watches → advisories, then soonest expiry.
- Summary chips (Extreme / Severe / Other) and filters (Warnings, Watches, Advisories).
- An "All clear 🍑" empty state.
- **Detail page:**
  - Severity header
  - A yellow **TAKE SHELTER NOW** card for tornado warnings
  - A polygon map with your location
  - "What to do" instructions
  - Full description and metadata
  - A link to the matching safety guide
  - Sharing
- **Notifications.** A local notification fires for new alerts at or above your chosen severity (default: Severe). Banners show even while the app is open.

## 🌀 Tropics

- **Season progress bar.** June 1 → Nov 30, with the Sep 10 peak marked and days remaining.
- **Active storms.** From the NHC, Atlantic basin only. Includes a satellite map with forecast cones and a card per storm.
- **Storm detail:**
  - Map with cone, track and category-colored forecast points
  - Winds, pressure, movement, distance from you
  - **Threat guidance** ("High threat" / "Be ready" / "Monitor") based on distance
  - Forecast track table
  - Link to the official NHC advisory
- **Before a storm hits.** A timeline from 5 days out to the evacuation order.
- **Know Your Zone.** Georgia's six coastal counties (Chatham, Bryan, Liberty, McIntosh, Glynn, Camden) with typical inland routes, plus links to GEMA/HS, Georgia 511 and the NHC.
- **Interactive Saffir-Simpson scale.** Tap a category 1–5 to see its wind range and expected damage.
- The **Demo Mode** toggle appears right here when the tropics are quiet.

## 🧰 Be Ready

- **Readiness Score:** a ring gauge combining kit progress, family plan completeness and whether alerts are on.
- **Power Outages** (simulated):
  - **Report form:**
    - Current location or address
    - Utility (Georgia Power / EMC / municipal)
    - Cause chips
    - A red "stay 30 feet away / call 911" warning for downed lines
    - Neighbors-affected toggle, optional photo, notes, phone, updates opt-in
  - **Submission:** an animated confirmation with a reference number such as `GA-482913`.
  - **Tracking:** a stage timeline (Received → Assessing → Crew assigned → Restoring → Restored) that advances with time.
  - **Outage center:** statewide total, a by-county breakdown, and pins on the radar map.
- **Emergency Kit:** 27 items in 6 categories (Ready.gov basics plus Georgia extras such as bug spray and a cooler), with progress and haptics.
- **Family Plan:**
  - Household members
  - Tornado shelter spot
  - Two meeting places
  - Out-of-town contact
  - Evacuation destination
  - Pet plan
  - Share the whole plan as formatted text
- **Safety Guides:** Hurricanes, Tornadoes, Flash Floods ("Turn Around, Don't Drown"), Extreme Heat, Ice & Winter Storms, Thunderstorms & Lightning, Power Outages & Generators.
- **Lightning Timer:** tap FLASH when you see lightning and BOOM when you hear thunder. It computes the distance (about 5 seconds per mile) and warns you inside the danger zone.
- **Flashlight & SOS:** steady torch, Morse **SOS** pattern (· · · — — — · · ·), or a bright-screen light for devices without a flash.
- **Emergency Contacts:** 911, Georgia 211, Georgia 511, Poison Control, GEMA/HS, NHC, NWS Peachtree City, Ready.gov. Tap to call or open.
- **"I'm Safe":** shares a check-in message with your general location through any messaging app.

## ⚙️ Settings
