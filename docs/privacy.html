Privacy Policy

Tufar Weather for iPhone · Effective September 26, 2026

The short version

Tufar Weather has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not run servers that receive your data. Your location is used only to get the weather where you are.

Location

If you allow location access, the app uses your location only while you are using it. It shows local conditions, radar around you, and your distance from alerts and storms.

To get your forecast and air quality, the app sends your approximate coordinates (rounded to about 10 meters) to Open-Meteo, a third-party weather service. No name, account, device identifier or other personal information is sent with them. Open-Meteo handles requests under its own privacy policy.

You can deny location access and choose a Georgia city from the list instead. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings › Tufar Weather › Location.

Other services the app contacts

The app downloads public data from the services below. These requests do not include your location or any personal information.

ServiceWhat the app requests
National Weather ServiceActive alerts for the state of Georgia
National Hurricane CenterCurrently active tropical storms
RainViewerRadar map images for the area you are viewing

Like any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address.

Data stored on your iPhone

Your settings, chosen city, emergency kit checklist, family emergency plan and outage reports are stored only on your iPhone. They are never uploaded. Deleting the app deletes them.

Outage reporting is currently a demonstration feature. Reports are not sent to any utility or to us.

Permissions

  • Location is used as described above.
  • Notifications are created on your device when a new severe weather alert matches your settings. We do not use push notifications or collect notification tokens.
  • Camera access is used only to turn on the flash for the flashlight and SOS feature. The app never takes photos or video.

Sharing

When you share something from the app, such as an alert, your family plan or an "I'm Safe" message, it goes through the standard iOS share sheet to the people and apps you choose. We do not receive a copy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
