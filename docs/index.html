Acme Weather

Georgia's storm companion, from the Blue Ridge to the Golden Isles.

Features Support Privacy Policy

Features

☀️ Now

Current conditions over an animated sky, a 24-hour strip, a 7-day forecast, the Muggy Meter, UV, air quality and pollen.

🗺️ Radar

Animated precipitation radar with NWS warning polygons, hurricane cones and power outages.

⚠️ Alerts

Live National Weather Service watches and warnings for Georgia, with notifications for the ones that matter.

🌀 Tropics

Active Atlantic storms from the National Hurricane Center, threat guidance and Know Your Zone evacuation routes.

🧰 Be Ready

Emergency kit checklist, family plan, safety guides, a lightning timer, a flashlight with SOS, and emergency contacts.

Support

Acme Weather is not an official warning system. In an emergency, call 911 and follow instructions from local officials and the National Weather Service.
